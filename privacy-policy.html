Privacy Policy — Qarz Daftar

Application: Qarz Daftar | Developer: Alimardon Toshmatov | Effective Date: October 17, 2026

🛡️
Summary of Our Privacy Commitment:

Qarz Daftar is built on a strict 100% offline, privacy-by-design architecture. All your debt records, financial transactions, contacts, and personal information stay exclusively on your local device. We do not operate external database servers, collect telemetry, track your location, or share your data with any third parties.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Qarz Daftar does not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or social logins to function. When using the app, you may input the following data:

  • Debt & Transaction Details: Borrower or lender names, transaction amounts, currency codes (UZS, USD, EUR, RUB), transaction dates, due dates, notes, and partial payment history.
  • Contact References: Phone numbers or contact names you manually type to associate with a debt entry. The app does not upload your device contacts to any server.
  • User Profile: Optional display name, phone number, and avatar image you set locally within the app's profile screen.

Storage Location: All of the above data is stored exclusively in a local SQLite (Room) database on your physical device.

2. No Remote Servers and No Data Transmission

We do not own, operate, or maintain any cloud servers or remote databases for Qarz Daftar. Your data never leaves your device over the Internet. The application functions completely offline without requiring an active network connection.

3. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

Qarz Daftar requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver its core features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): On Android 13 (API level 33) and higher, this permission is requested solely to deliver local reminder notifications for upcoming debt due dates. All reminders are scheduled locally using Android's system AlarmManager/WorkManager. No remote push notifications or device push tokens are used.
  • Storage / Document Access (SAF): When you explicitly choose to create a backup (Export) or restore from an existing file (Import), Android's system Storage Access Framework is used to write or read a local JSON file on your storage. The app does not access other files on your device.

4. Security and Biometric Authentication

To protect your financial privacy, Qarz Daftar includes optional device lock features:

  • 4-Digit PIN Lock: Your PIN is stored securely in encrypted local storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences/Keystore) on your device.
  • Biometric Unlock (Fingerprint / Face): Authentication is handled entirely by the Android OS using the official BiometricPrompt API. Biometric verification occurs strictly within your device's hardware Secure Element / Trusted Execution Environment (TEE). Qarz Daftar never accesses, views, or stores your biometric data.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

To ensure absolute privacy:

  • We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs (e.g. AdMob, Unity, AppLovin).
  • We do not include third-party analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK).
  • We do not use tracking cookies or persistent device identifiers for advertising purposes.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data resides entirely on your device, you have complete control:

  • You can edit or delete individual debt entries or payment logs at any time inside the app.
  • You can reset all application data by clearing app storage in Android Settings > Apps > Qarz Daftar > Storage > Clear Data, or by simply uninstalling the application. This immediately and irreversibly deletes all stored records from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Qarz Daftar does not target or knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe a child has provided personal information within the app, note that all data remains solely on that device and can be cleared via app settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Qarz Daftar ilovasi qat'iy 100% offline va maxfiy arxitektura asosida yaratilgan. Barcha qarzlar, to'lovlar, kontaktlar va shaxsiy ma'lumotlar faqatgina o'zingizning telefoningizda saqlanadi. Biz hech qanday tashqi serverlarga ma'lumot yubormaymiz, sizni kuzatmaymiz va ma'lumotlaringizni uchinchi shaxslarga bermaymiz.

1. Qaysi ma'lumotlar saqlanadi?

Qarz Daftar ilovasidan foydalanish uchun ro'yxatdan o'tish, telefon raqami yoki elektron pochtani kiritish talab qilinmaydi. Ilovadan foydalanish jarayonida siz kiritishingiz mumkin bo'lgan ma'lumotlar:

  • Qarz va to'lov ma'lumotlari: Qarz oluvchi yoki beruvchi ismi, summasi, valyuta turi (UZS, USD, EUR, RUB), olingan/berilgan sana, qaytarish muddati, izohlar va qisman to'lovlar tarixi.
  • Kontakt ma'lumotlari: Qarz yozuvi uchun siz kiritgan ism yoki telefon raqami. Ilova kontaktlar ro'yxatingizni hech qayerga yuklamaydi.
  • Foydalanuvchi profili: Profil sahifasida o'zingiz kiritgan ixtiyoriy ism, telefon raqami va rasm.

Saqlash joyi: Ushbu ma'lumotlarning barchasi faqat sizning qurilmangizning ichki SQLite (Room) ma'lumotlar bazasida saqlanadi.

2. Serverlarning yo'qligi va Offline rejim

Qarz Daftar ilovasi uchun hech qanday tashqi server yoki bulutli ma'lumotlar bazasi mavjud emas. Ma'lumotlaringiz Internet orqali tashqariga chiqmaydi. Ilova internet tarmog'isiz (100% offline) to'liq ishlaydi.

3. Qurilma ruxsatlari (Permissions)

Ilova faqat o'z funksiyalari uchun zarur bo'lgan minimal ruxsatlarni so'raydi:

  • Bildirishnomalar (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Android 13 va undan yuqori tizimlarda qarzni qaytarish muddati kelganda mahalliy eslatma yuborish uchun so'raladi. Eslatmalar to'liq qurilmaning ichki mexanizmi orqali ishlaydi, tashqi push-serverlar ishlatilmaydi.
  • Xotiraga kirish (Storage Access Framework): Faqat siz o'zingiz zaxira nusxa yaratganingizda (Eksport) yoki zaxira faylidan tiklaganingizda (Import) JSON faylni saqlash yoki o'qish uchun foydalaniladi.

4. Xavfsizlik va Biometrik himoya

Moliyaviy ma'lumotlaringiz xavfsizligini ta'minlash uchun ilovada quyidagi himoya vositalari mavjud:

  • 4 xonali PIN-kod: PIN-kod qurilmaning shifrlangan xotirasida (Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences) saqlanadi.
  • Biometriya (Barmoq izi / Face ID): Autentifikatsiya to'liq Android operatsion tizimining rasmiy BiometricPrompt xizmati orqali amalga oshiriladi. Ilova sizning barmoq izingiz yoki yuz ma'lumotlaringizni ko'rmaydi va saqlamaydi.

5. Reklama va Tahlil (Analytics) xizmatlari yo'qligi

To'liq maxfiylikni kafolatlash maqsadida:

  • Ilovada hech qanday reklama tarmoqlari (AdMob, Unity va h.k.) mavjud emas.
  • Hech qanday tashqi kuzatuv yoki tahlil tizimlari (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK) o'rnatilmagan.

6. Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish va nazorat qilish

Barcha ma'lumotlar o'z qurilmangizda bo'lgani sababli, ularni to'liq o'zingiz nazorat qilasiz:
